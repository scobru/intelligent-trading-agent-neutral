  - asset: an asset from the funding table that you do not hold yet.
  - target_portion_of_portfolio: fraction of TOTAL portfolio value to commit

Judgement:
- The only income is funding. A pair is worth opening only when shorts have
  been paid consistently, not just in this cycle: look at the AVERAGE APR
  and its minimum, and at how many observations it is based on.
- Your own short dilutes the funding: the table shows the rate before you
  enter, the executor computes it after. Crowded long markets (long OI much
  larger than short OI) pay shorts the most.
- Opening and closing cost swap fees, perp fees and gas. Churning a pair
  for a few points of APR loses money: once open, keep it while funding
  stays positive on average.
- Close when funding has turned and stayed negative, or when the pair's
  effective leverage is climbing (the short loses margin when price rises,
  while the spot gains in the wallet).
- Idle USDC earns nothing, but idle is better than a pair that pays shorts
  nothing. "hold" is a valid and often correct answer.
- If pairs were closed automatically this cycle, do not reopen the same
  asset right away.
